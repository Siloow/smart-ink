How to inspect this test

Click a body panel to move the anchor. A seamless chart keeps the grid continuous when it crosses an atlas split. A non-warping chart keeps grid squares and text consistent on the surface. A planar projection becomes stretched as the surface turns away; its mirrored copy can appear on another side.
